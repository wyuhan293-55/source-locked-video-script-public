# Material Analysis

Use this reference to decide **what is worth cutting, what decision the viewer is trying to make, what evidence can move that decision, and how the selected source should move forward**.

## 1. Decision Progress Gate

For medical-aesthetics doctor IP content, content value is **decision progress, not knowledge density**.

A piece is worth making when it meaningfully moves the target viewer on at least one decision job:

- `result_projection`: do I want this kind of result / can I picture myself in it?
- `fit_judgment`: does this direction fit a foundation like mine / what should I judge first?
- `professional_trust`: does this doctor show judgment, restraint, or capability I can trust?
- `risk_reduction`: is a concrete fear, uncertainty, recovery concern, or failure risk made clearer?
- `decision_continuation`: after watching, does the viewer have a more specific next question worth resolving?

A piece does **not** need to teach a new medical fact to be valuable. A real result, strong case, recovery feedback, or credential/trust proof can be valuable when it advances one of the decision jobs above.

Failure condition:

- the viewer learns facts but is no closer to wanting, judging, trusting, de-risking, or continuing the decision.

## 2. Evidence Form Is Not Content Job

Do not classify the content only by what the footage contains.

Common evidence forms include:

- result / before-after / state change
- case story
- doctor judgment
- technical or procedural explanation
- restraint / refusal / tradeoff
- credential or experience proof
- consultation interaction
- recovery feedback
- user reaction or testimonial

These are **evidence forms**, not automatically the content's purpose.

First decide the decision job. Then choose the strongest available evidence to complete it.

Examples:

- result footage may serve `result_projection` or `professional_trust`
- technical explanation may serve `professional_trust` or `risk_reduction`
- consultation judgment may serve `fit_judgment`
- recovery feedback may serve `risk_reduction`

Do not force every piece into education merely because doctor explanation exists.

## 3. Choose The Viewing Task Before The Structure

Identify the one unresolved question the viewer is watching to resolve.

For medical-aesthetics content, phrase it in user decision language when possible:

- 做完会是什么感觉？
- 这种基础到底该看什么？
- 为什么这个结果能成立？
- 这个医生为什么值得信？
- 我最担心的风险由什么决定？
- 看完以后，我自然还会追问什么？

Do not default to pain, biography, doctor explanation, case review, or knowledge teaching.

Useful tensions often come from:

- user belief vs doctor correction
- desired result vs foundation limit
- surface problem vs real judgment focus
- wanting more vs doctor restraint
- previous experience vs new decision standard
- expectation vs source-backed result/reaction

These are relationships, not templates.

## 4. Hook Selection

A strong opening creates an unresolved question quickly and makes the target user want the answer.

Prefer:

- a concrete result or change that creates desire or curiosity
- a specific user worry or desire
- a contradiction
- a surprising doctor judgment
- a refusal/restraint line
- a source-backed reaction that makes the viewer ask why
- a credential/trust fact only when it materially lowers decision risk
- a relationship or identity detail only when it sharpens the decision conflict

Usually use as support, not the opening:

- greetings and arrival
- age/city/job without conflict
- `做了很多功课`
- pure praise
- long technical explanation before the viewer knows why it matters

## 5. Structure Rule: Change The Viewer's Decision State

Every next selected fragment must change what the viewer currently knows, expects, trusts, fears, or needs answered.

Valid movement includes:

- 不知道结果 -> 看见具体结果
- 原以为 A -> 发现 B
- 想要 A -> 发现有限制
- 看见问题 -> 知道判断重点
- 知道判断重点 -> 知道怎么取舍
- 不确定医生能力 -> 看见具体判断/证据
- 担心风险 -> 知道风险由什么决定
- 听到判断 -> 得到结果/反馈/事实确认

If the next fragment only:

- repeats the same symptom
- restates the same judgment
- changes wording but not meaning
- adds another praise line
- lists another plan without changing the decision
- adds technical knowledge that does not help the viewing task

cut it.

A different source clip does **not** count as new information by itself.

## 6. Doctor Material: Judgment Before Education

Prioritize the doctor's actual:

- judgment
- correction
- refusal
- restraint
- choice
- tradeoff

Then keep only the explanation needed to understand **why that judgment matters to the viewer's decision**.

Do not turn a doctor monologue into a knowledge lecture when one or two judgment lines plus evidence already complete the decision job.

## 7. Result / Trust Material

Do not downgrade result display, credentials, recovery feedback, or real trust proof simply because they contain less teaching.

Keep them when they perform a clear decision function:

- result creates desire or self-projection
- result verifies a prior judgment
- recovery feedback lowers a concrete fear
- credential/experience proof lowers uncertainty
- interaction demonstrates restraint or trustworthiness

Cut them when they are only empty praise, status display, or self-congratulation.

## 8. Background And Story Material

Use story/context only when it does at least one job:

- explains why the decision matters now
- sharpens the conflict
- changes the decision standard
- lowers a real trust barrier
- makes the later professional judgment or result more meaningful

If removing the story does not reduce decision value or tension, cut it.

## 9. Stop Rule

Stop when the viewing task and primary decision job have landed.

Do not add an abstract summary, generic praise, extra plan detail, knowledge recap, or template ending merely to make the script feel structurally complete.

## 10. Failure Patterns

- `痛点 -> 同义痛点`
- `背景 -> 背景 -> 项目`
- `医生知识 -> 医生知识`
- `判断 -> 同义判断`
- `方案 -> 重复方案`
- `夸赞 -> 夸赞`
- technically informative but does not move the user's decision
- story is complete but the viewer is no closer to wanting, trusting, judging, or acting
- result is shown but the viewer cannot tell why it matters
- the title asks a broader question than the source can answer

## 11. Output Mode Selection

### 医生讲解顺剪版

Use when the source itself already contains a clear judgment path and only needs trimming/reordering.

### 小红书包装成片版

Use when useful source fragments are scattered and need reordering or clearly labeled non-source packaging to make the viewing task understandable.

### 不建议出稿

Use when the proposed topic depends on a cause, diagnosis, result, credential, guarantee, or broad rule that the source does not support or the target channel does not allow.

When unsure, ask:

1. What decision job does this piece complete?
2. What is the strongest evidence for that job?
3. Does the source itself answer the viewing task?
4. Can the script be completed without inventing a bridge?
5. Does every major beat move the viewer's decision state?

If not, narrow, merge, or do not draft.
